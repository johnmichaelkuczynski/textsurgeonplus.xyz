PART 2 — NODE LEGITIMACY RULE (applies to every tier 3 and deeper, manual or auto)

6. THE RULE (include verbatim in every tier-generation prompt)
"Every node must ESTABLISH its parent, by evidence or by logic:
 (a) EMPIRICAL: a specific fact, measurement, experiment, observed case, or real-world instance, stated concretely (what, where, when, how much).
 (b) LOGICAL: a derivation, a demonstration, or a counterexample, stated in full.
 A node that reports what someone SAYS, ARGUES, or BELIEVES establishes nothing and is forbidden. A person may appear only as part of a fact (e.g. 'In 1847 Semmelweis cut maternal deaths in his ward from about 18% to about 2% by requiring handwashing'), never as an authority (e.g. 'Semmelweis argued that handwashing matters').
 Vague appeals are forbidden: 'studies show', 'research indicates', 'a study found', 'experts agree', 'it is widely accepted', 'a classic illustration', 'a well-known example'.
 The node must support its parent's EXACT claim, not a neighboring claim that shares a word with it.
 Citations are allowed only as a footnote marker (¹, ², ...) at the end of a factual node. The citation never replaces the fact."

7. ENFORCEMENT — TWO CHECKS ON EVERY NEW NODE, BEFORE DISPLAY
Check A (pattern filter, in code). Reject any node containing, case-insensitively:
  argued, argues, posits, posited, contends, maintains that, claims that, according to, as noted in, as discussed in, is discussed in, highlights, emphasizes, suggests that, states that, notes that, the article, the paper, the book, the encyclopedia, Stanford Encyclopedia, Internet Encyclopedia, SEP, IEP, studies show, research indicates, research shows, a study found, experts, widely accepted, classic illustration, well-known example.
Check B (model classifier, one call per batch). For each node return one label and a one-line reason:
  EMPIRICAL | LOGICAL | AUTHORITY | VAGUE | OFF-TARGET
  Prompt: "Label each node. EMPIRICAL = a specific, concrete fact or case. LOGICAL = a derivation, demonstration, or counterexample. AUTHORITY = its content is that someone says/argues/believes something. VAGUE = gestures at evidence without stating it. OFF-TARGET = does not establish the parent's exact claim. Return: node number, label, reason."
A node passes only if it passes Check A AND Check B labels it EMPIRICAL or LOGICAL.

8. REGENERATION
- Regenerate each failed node once with: "Your node was rejected as <label>: <reason>. Replace it with a node that establishes the parent by a concrete fact or by logic."
- Recheck. If it still fails, drop it and show under the tree: "Node <number> dropped: <label> — <reason>."
- If a parent is left with no children, show: "Node <parent number> has no legitimate support."

9. CITATIONS
- Footnote markers only, at the end of factual nodes; sources listed under "Sources", keyed by marker and node number.
- A node whose only content is a citation fails as AUTHORITY.

10. SCOPE
- Tiers 1–2 (distilled from the author's text) are exempt.
- User-typed instructions do NOT switch this rule off unless they explicitly say "allow citation nodes".
